Tiltwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tiltwise ("the game") handles, and why. The game is published by KryoTale Studio ("we").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to deliver and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, coins, lives, purchased shop items and settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If device backup is enabled in your system settings, your operating system may include it in your own device backup.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • ad interactions and diagnostic information (such as ad impressions, clicks and crash data).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice by clearing the game's data and opening it again.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You only see one when you choose to.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers. To remove the data stored on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data. For data held by Google, use the controls described in Google's policies above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
